Top Student & Visitor Destinations

Explore our special student holiday packages across Terengganu's most iconic spots:

1. Pulau Redang Marine Park

Famous for crystal-clear turquoise waters, white sandy beaches, and vibrant coral reefs ideal for snorkelling and diving trips. Includes 3D2N chalet stay and boat transfer.

Promotion Price: RM 299 RM 199 per pax

Learn more at the Official Terengganu Tourism Portal.

2. Kuala Terengganu Drawbridge & Pasar Payang

Experience the iconic Drawbridge skyline, shop for traditional batik and songket at Pasar Payang, and savour authentic Keropok Lekor and Nasi Dagang by the waterfront.

Promotion Price: RM 150 RM 99 per pax

3. Tasik Kenyir Eco-Adventure & Islamic Civilization Park

Discover Southeast Asia's largest man-made lake, rainforest waterfalls, and the famous Crystal Mosque at Taman Tamadun Islam.

Promotion Price: RM 220 RM 149 per pax
